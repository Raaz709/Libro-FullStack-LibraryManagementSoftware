12. Important frontend architecture

I would not make every page independently call fetch().

Instead:

UI
 ↓
custom hook
 ↓
API service
 ↓
backend

For example:

pages/Books
      ↓
useBooks()
      ↓
bookApi
      ↓
/api/Books

That will keep the frontend maintainable as the project grows.
